Before weekly waits; rounded up
Model. Five-hour-limited weekly throughput = 5h capacity × 168 ÷ (5 + dwell hours). Effective throughput = the lower of that value and the weekly capacity. Minimum dwell sets dwell to zero. Consistent dwell uses the observed median. The current mandatory wait is then added once to the ETA.

Interpretation: this is an allowance-constrained calendar forecast, not a promise about engineering effort. It becomes meaningful only after the project has enough measured sessions to estimate remaining credits and empirical capacity. If the weekly ceiling is the bottleneck, reducing dwell below that point will not make the project finish faster.

OpenAI currently states that Work/Codex may be limited by both a five-hour window and a weekly window, and allowance must remain in both; limits vary by plan. Usage-limit reference.
